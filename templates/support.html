Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order?

After placing an order, log in with your phone number and go to "My Orders". You'll see live status updates from the farmer and courier.

What if my order doesn't arrive?

If the farmer doesn't confirm within 30 minutes, your order is automatically cancelled and refunded. For other issues, contact us via WhatsApp or email.

How do I become a farmer on SokoShamba?

Contact us via WhatsApp or email and we'll guide you through the verification process. Once verified, you can start listing your produce.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. We use M‑Pesa's secure STK Push system. Your money is held safely until your order is delivered. We never store your M‑Pesa PIN.
